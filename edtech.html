Frequently Asked Questions

Here are the answers to some of the most commonly asked questions related to education technology.

What is ERP?

ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) helps manage and automate administrative and academic processes within an institution.

What is Admission CRM?

Admission CRM helps educational institutions manage student inquiries, applications, and the complete admission process efficiently.

What is LMS?

LMS (Learning Management System) enables institutions to deliver, track, and manage online learning courses and training programs.

What are the benefits of partnering with EdTech Innovate?

EdTech Innovate provides advanced tools to streamline management, improve learning outcomes, and enhance communication between stakeholders.

How does EdTech Innovate help in conducting exams?

It offers online and offline exam solutions with secure systems, real-time monitoring, and automated result generation.
